Kyoto, Japan

A journey through timeless streets and temples

Overview Culture Food Travel Tips

Overview

Kyoto served as the capital of Japan for over a thousand years and remains one of the country's cultural treasures, home to thousands of temples, shrines, and traditional wooden houses.

Culture & Heritage

From the geisha district of Gion to the golden pavilion of Kinkaku-ji, Kyoto preserves centuries of tradition while quietly embracing modern life.

Local Cuisine

Kyoto's kaiseki cuisine is a multi-course dining experience built around seasonal ingredients, delicate presentation, and centuries-old technique.

Travel Tips

Visit in early spring for cherry blossoms or in autumn for vivid red maple leaves. Renting a bicycle is one of the best ways to explore the older neighborhoods at your own pace.
